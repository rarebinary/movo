## Reference boundary

Movo may study and independently reproduce the **interaction grammar** visible here: immersive media hierarchy, continuous edge-to-edge composition, window geometry, compact control proportions, anchored popovers, sectional rhythm, and state clarity.

Movo must not copy Wallspace's logo, name, wallpaper imagery, copywriting, icons, catalogue, PRO/community features, exact navigation, exact measurements, proprietary implementation, or source code. Movo remains a single local workspace with its own chrome-and-black identity.

## Movo translation

- Use a continuous `Void Black → Graphite` canvas with no differently colored side gutters.
- Keep native traffic lights, resizing, full screen, accessibility, and keyboard behavior.
- Increase the apparent outer radius to the `22–24pt` family and clip all content consistently to that geometry.
- Let the selected video fill the stage and extend visually behind the top instrument row when space permits.
- Group related controls into dark translucent wells; use compact circular or rounded-rectangle hit targets with one shared geometry system.
- Keep polished chrome scarce: Movo mark, selected state, and **Set Wallpaper** only.
- Move target/display choice into a small action-anchored popover; keep advanced framing and loop controls in the retractable inspector.
- Keep Movo's filmstrip and single-workspace topology. Do not add Wallspace's Home/Explore/Library navigation to V1.

## Visual validation

Every visual iteration should be captured at `1240 × 790pt`, the minimum `960 × 640pt`, and full screen. Compare composition, not content. Confirm Reduced Motion, Increased Contrast, keyboard focus, long names, empty library, importing, applying, success, and error states before calling the redesign complete.
